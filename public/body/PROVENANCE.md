# Mannequin v2

`mannequin-v2.json` is generated by `npm run body:generate` from the MakeHuman `hm08` base mesh, a realistic, neutral human figure with modelled face, ears, fingers, and toes.

- Source: MakeHuman base mesh, commit `3c701a8e52f09e69922e8b598d23be2d7dfc49e3` of https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman (`makehuman/data/3dobjs/base.obj`), checked in unchanged at `scripts/body/source/makehuman-hm08-base.obj` (SHA-256 `8e761e6624b8f54536409135d1636da63b32486a90d4897f84e121d144f6fb4c`).
- Source license: CC0 1.0 Universal. The file header states it "was explicitly released as CC0 in september 2020"; MakeHuman's `LICENSE.md` lists "the base mesh and proxies" among assets released under CC0. Full text: `scripts/body/source/LICENSE-CC0.md`. No attribution is required; we credit MakeHuman (Data Collection AB, Joel Palmius, Jonas Hauquier) anyway.
- Kept: the `body` skin and the two eyeball helpers. Discarded: hair, clothing, genital, teeth, tongue, and eyelash helpers, so the figure is a smooth, neutral mannequin. Joint helpers are read only to locate the skeleton.
- Processing (this repository, `scripts/body/`): scaled to 1.96 m with feet at y = 0, +Z front, +X patient-left; quads split along the shorter diagonal; 45 patient-relative regions labelled from the skeleton and facial landmarks (`regions.ts`); region-aligned, fold-free UV charts packed into the 512x512 atlas at about 4.5 mm per texel with 5 px gutters (`charts.ts`); area-weighted smooth normals; landmarks and camera focus presets; and the 2D diagram traced from orthographic renders of the same region labels (`diagram.ts`).
- Also generated: `region-atlas-mannequin-v2.png` (inspection copy, 0 = invalid, otherwise `REGION_IDS` index + 1), `src/body/region-raster-data.ts` (the same raster, run-length encoded for the shared reducer), and `src/body/diagram-data.ts`.

## Default figure, body shapes, and appearance

- **Default figure:** the displayed body is MakeHuman's own default person: the base mesh plus the equal-ethnicity average of the young-adult female and male shapes (sex 0.5). It is applied to positions only. Regions and UV charts are computed on the base mesh, so maps and measurements are unchanged.
- **Body-shape sliders:** `shape-mannequin-v2.json` holds 24 compact morphs derived by `npm run body:shape` from 38 CC0 MakeHuman target files at the same pinned commit. Each file's SHA-256 is recorded in the pack, and each file declares its CC0 release. The morphs cover sex, age, muscle, weight, height, waist, shoulder width, hip width, and leg length. Breast, genital, and ethnicity controls are not used. The female side of the sex slider is capped at 35% over the breasts (full within 10 cm of each nipple, fading smoothly to none by 22 cm, so the neck and shoulders have no seam), and the height and leg targets use a realistic part of their range.
- **Smooth chest:** the base mesh and its sex/age targets model nipples. Both generators pass the base positions and every shape morph through one fixed linear filter (`scripts/body/chest.ts`): an area-weighted Gaussian blur over true surface distance (σ = 1 cm), applied fully within 1.5 cm of each nipple and fading out by 4 cm. It removes centimetre-scale bumps but keeps the broader chest curve. Because the filter is linear, every slider blend is smooth too. The nipple area is located by source vertex index, using the vertices moved by MakeHuman's CC0 `breast/nipple-point` and `breast/nipple-size` targets at the pinned commit. Those targets are never applied. Only positions change, so triangles, UVs, regions, and saved maps are unaffected.
- **Appearance:** a plain neutral grey figure with no skin tone and no clothing.

Body-map version: `mannequin-v2`. Maps saved for `mannequin-v1` are rejected as incompatible rather than reinterpreted. Any change to the source, region rules, charts, or packing requires a new version. Measurements are map coverage of this model's surface, not anatomy.

Audit: `tests/body.test.ts` checks that the asset, raster, and diagram reproduce from the pinned source; that charts are disjoint and no texel is covered twice; outward winding and seam-consistent normals; patient sides; region capacity; focus presets; and diagram coverage. Painting behaviour is tested in `tests/body/`.
